Skin microbiome · 16S findings

What the skin community is telling us

What our 16S data shows, what it means clinically, and what it can’t yet say

16S rRNA · bacteria, genus/species · ≈200 sequenced, 93 with full quiz · cross-sectional · associations, not diagnoses

Four axes the data can speak to

Barrier integrity

resident vs. transient

C. acnes + S. epidermidis dominance vs. the load of oral/environmental genera (Streptococcus, Neisseria, Haemophilus, Prevotella). Rising transients = a disturbed or permeable surface.

Most defensible dysbiosis readout.

Staph defense balance

atopic / barrier

S. epidermidis (makes antimicrobials, suppresses C. acnes) vs. the S. aureus-group (pathobiont, drives eczema flares). Their ratio is the atopic axis.

Lever: epidermidis ÷ aureus as a defense score.

Corynebacterium shift

sensitivity-adjacent

Lipophilic Coryne species (incl. C. kroppenstedtii) track sebaceous, occluded, sometimes inflamed or odor-prone skin.

Lever: a flag for sensitive / reactive profiles.

Perturbation signature

community disruption

Antibiotics, antibacterial soaps and harsh actives knock down the dominant commensals; secondary Cutibacterium & Staph species expand and diversity drops.

Lever: flag antimicrobial overuse as a stressor.

Strongest signals, by confidence

A

Trust it — mechanism + scans agree

Recent antibiotics ↑ C. avidum; antibacterial soap ↑ S. warneri; antibiotics & eczema ↓ diversity — the perturbation axis. Irritation ↑ Neisseria; rosacea ↓ overall scan & ↓ moisture — the barrier axis.

B

Worth chasing — plausible leads

Physical scrubs ↓ richness. Diet: carbs ↓ Staph, processed food ↑ redness, meat ↑ S. hominis. Sunscreen ↑ oral Strep, ↓ S. capitis.

C

Discount — noise or reverse-causal

Sparse-taxa hits (Dolosigranulum, Finegoldia, Moraxella). “Product → clearer skin” is reverse-causal — people buy the product because of their skin.

A behaviour-and-barrier map of the bacterial skin community — strong on perturbation and barrier signals, deliberately silent on acne-strain and fungal questions until the assay can see them.

What 16S can’t tell us

  • Acne is a strain story — not callable from C. acnes % (no phylotypes).
  • Malassezia is invisible — fungal; no “fungal acne” or seb. derm.
  • No clean S. aureus call — only an “aureus-group” flag.
  • No function — sebum, pH, antimicrobial peptides only inferred.
  • On the face, low diversity is healthy — don’t read “more = better.”

Path to clinical

  • Shotgun metagenomics → C. acnes phylotypes + strain-level aureus (unlocks acne).
  • ITS sequencing → Malassezia (unlocks sensitivity / seb. derm).
  • Longitudinal / before–after → turns associations into causes.
  • Healthy reference panel → a true “distance-from-healthy” dysbiosis score.
